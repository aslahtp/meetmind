# Code of Conduct

MeetMind follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, welcome newcomers, assume good intent, and keep discussions focused on the project. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/aslahtp). Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer may remove content or block participants who violate this code.
