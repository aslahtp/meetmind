1What it is

What is MeetMind?

MeetMind is a free, open-source desktop app for Windows and macOS that turns online meetings into written meeting notes. It records your computer's system audio and your microphone together, transcribes the recording with the speech-to-text service you choose, and uses Google Gemini, Groq or Anthropic Claude to write a speaker-labelled summary with decisions and action items.

Because recording happens on your own computer, nothing joins the call as a participant, and it works with any app that plays sound: Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Slack, Discord or a file you already have. Notes stay in a local database and can be exported to PDF, copied as Markdown or synced to Notion.

2How it works

From call to minutes in four steps

Start a recording however suits you. When you stop, MeetMind does the rest.

  1. 1

    Record

    Click the MeetMind button in Google Meet or Zoom, press Record in the app, or accept the reminder when a calendar meeting starts.

  2. 2

    Transcribe

    AssemblyAI, Google Cloud Speech-to-Text, Sarvam AI or Groq returns a transcript (speaker-labelled, except Groq).

  3. 3

    Summarize

    Gemini, Groq or Claude writes an executive summary, agenda, key decisions and an action items table with owners and deadlines.

  4. 4

    Share

    Search every meeting's notes and transcript in the app, export a PDF, copy Markdown, or sync a formatted page to Notion.

System audio + microphone

Records both sides of any call: through WASAPI loopback on Windows and Core Audio on macOS, with FFmpeg bundled or installed from inside the app.

Your choice of speech-to-text

AssemblyAI, Google Cloud Speech-to-Text v1/v2, Sarvam AI or Groq Whisper, each with a model picker. Speaker diarization on all but Groq.

7FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is MeetMind free?

Yes. MeetMind is free and open source under the MIT license. You pay only for the AI services you connect, and Gemini and Groq (free tiers) and AssemblyAI (signup credit) can be used at no cost for moderate use.

Does a bot join my meeting?

No. MeetMind records the audio playing on your own computer plus your microphone, so no extra participant appears in Google Meet, Zoom or Teams.

Which meeting apps does MeetMind work with?

Any app that plays audio on your computer, including Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Slack, Discord and Webex. On Windows, for Google Meet, Zoom and Teams, MeetMind detects the call and offers to start recording; the optional Chrome extension adds a record button inside Meet and Zoom tabs.

Does MeetMind work on macOS or Linux?

macOS: yes, as a beta for macOS 14.2 and later on Apple silicon and Intel Macs. It records system audio with the Core Audio capture added in macOS 14.2; meeting detection isn't available on macOS yet. Linux isn't supported.

Where is my meeting data stored?

Recordings, transcripts and notes are stored locally on your PC. There is no MeetMind server or account: audio is sent only to the speech-to-text provider you choose, and the transcript only to the note-generation provider you choose (Gemini, Groq or Claude), using your own API keys.

Which languages does MeetMind support?

Language support depends on the speech-to-text engine you choose. For meetings that mix English and Malayalam, Sarvam AI's saaras:v3 model handles code-switching within a sentence.

How is MeetMind different from Otter.ai or Fireflies.ai?

Hosted note takers such as Otter.ai and Fireflies.ai are subscription services that typically join calls as a bot and keep recordings on their servers. MeetMind is an open-source desktop app that records locally without a bot, lets you choose the transcription provider, stores notes on your machine, and costs nothing beyond your own API usage.

Can MeetMind make notes from a recording I already have?

Yes. Import an existing WAV, MP3, M4A, MP4, WebM or similar file, or paste a transcript to generate notes without speech-to-text.

Ready for your next meeting?

Install MeetMind, add a Gemini, Groq or Claude key and a speech-to-text key, and your next call becomes searchable minutes.
