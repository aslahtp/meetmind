1What it is

What is MeetMind?

MeetMind is a free, open-source desktop app for Windows that turns online meetings into written meeting notes. It records your computer's system audio and your microphone together, transcribes the recording with the speech-to-text service you choose, and uses Google Gemini to write a speaker-labelled summary with decisions and action items.

Because recording happens on your own PC, nothing joins the call as a participant, and it works with any app that plays sound: Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Slack, Discord or a file you already have. Notes stay in a local database and can be exported to PDF, copied as Markdown or synced to Notion.

2How it works

From call to minutes in four steps

  1. 1

    Record

    Click the MeetMind button in Google Meet or Zoom, press Record in the app, or accept the reminder when a calendar meeting starts.

  2. 2

    Transcribe

    AssemblyAI, Google Cloud Speech-to-Text or Sarvam AI returns a transcript with speaker labels.

  3. 3

    Summarize

    Gemini writes an executive summary, agenda, key decisions and an action items table with owners and deadlines.

  4. 4

    Share

    Search the notes in the app, export a PDF, copy Markdown, or sync a formatted page to Notion.

3Features

Everything a meeting note taker needs

System audio + microphone

Records both sides of any call on Windows through WASAPI loopback, with FFmpeg installed from inside the app.

Your choice of speech-to-text

AssemblyAI, Google Cloud Speech-to-Text v1/v2 or Sarvam AI, all with speaker diarization.

English–Malayalam code-switching

Accurate transcripts of mixed-language meetings with Sarvam AI's saaras:v3 model.

AI minutes with Gemini

Executive-style Markdown or structured JSON notes, an editable prompt, and an automatic fallback model.

Google Meet & Zoom extension

A floating Chrome overlay with one-click recording and live processing status.

Notion, PDF & Markdown

Native Notion pages with tables and to-dos, dated PDF exports, and one-click Markdown copy.

Google Calendar reminders

See upcoming meetings on the dashboard and get a prompt to start recording when one begins.

Local-first and private

No account and no MeetMind server. Recordings, transcripts and notes stay in a local SQLite database.

6FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is MeetMind free?

Yes. MeetMind is free and open source under the MIT license. You pay only for the AI services you connect, and both Gemini (free tier) and AssemblyAI (signup credit) can be used at no cost for moderate use.

Does a bot join my meeting?

No. MeetMind records the audio playing on your own computer plus your microphone, so no extra participant appears in Google Meet, Zoom or Teams.

Which meeting apps does MeetMind work with?

Any app that plays audio on Windows, including Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Slack, Discord and Webex. The Chrome extension adds a one-click record button for Google Meet and Zoom in the browser.

Does MeetMind work on macOS or Linux?

Not currently. MeetMind is built for Windows 10 and 11 because it relies on Windows audio loopback capture.

Where is my meeting data stored?

Recordings, transcripts and notes are stored locally on your PC. There is no MeetMind server or account: audio is sent only to the speech-to-text provider you choose, and the transcript only to Gemini, using your own API keys.

Which languages does MeetMind support?

Language support depends on the speech-to-text engine you choose. For meetings that mix English and Malayalam, Sarvam AI's saaras:v3 model handles code-switching within a sentence.

How is MeetMind different from Otter.ai or Fireflies.ai?

Hosted note takers such as Otter.ai and Fireflies.ai are subscription services that typically join calls as a bot and keep recordings on their servers. MeetMind is an open-source desktop app that records locally without a bot, lets you choose the transcription provider, stores notes on your machine, and costs nothing beyond your own API usage.

Can MeetMind make notes from a recording I already have?

Yes. Import an existing WAV, MP3, M4A, MP4, WebM or similar file, or paste a transcript to generate notes without speech-to-text.

Ready for your next meeting?

Install MeetMind, add a Gemini key and a speech-to-text key, and your next call becomes searchable minutes.
