Windows 10 & 11

AI meeting notes for Windows, free and open source

MeetMind is a Windows app that records any call with system audio and your microphone, notices when Teams, Zoom or Google Meet starts, and turns each meeting into a transcript and minutes.

Download for Windows Install steps

MeetMind-Setup-__VERSION__.exe · Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit · FFmpeg included

In short

MeetMind for Windows records the other participants through Windows loopback audio (WASAPI) and you through your microphone, so it works with any meeting app without a bot. It sits in the tray, offers to record when a call starts, and after the call writes a speaker-labelled transcript and minutes with decisions and action items, stored on your PC.

Install MeetMind on Windows

  1. Download MeetMind-Setup-__VERSION__.exe from the latest release and run it.
  2. The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC". Click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Open Settings › Transcription and add a speech-to-text key: AssemblyAI (recommended), Google Cloud Speech-to-Text, Sarvam AI or Groq. Every key field links to a guide for getting one.
  4. Open Settings › Notes and add a Gemini, Groq or Claude key, then pick a model. One free Groq key covers both transcription and notes.
  5. Optional: connect Notion and your calendars in Settings › Integrations.

FFmpeg, which MeetMind uses to convert recordings, is included in the installer.

Recording calls on Windows

MeetMind captures the audio your PC plays together with your microphone, so both sides of the call end up in one recording. That works with Microsoft Teams (new and classic), Zoom, Google Meet in any major browser, Slack, Discord, Webex and anything else that plays sound.

Automatic meeting detection

When Teams, Zoom or a browser with a Meet, Zoom or Teams tab starts using your microphone, a slim overlay unfolds at the top of the screen with a Record button. During the call it shows the timer; when the call ends it asks whether to stop, then shows processing progress. If you ignore the prompt for two minutes, it goes away for that meeting. Turn detection off in Settings › General.

Other ways to record

  • The Record button in the app, or the tray icon's menu.
  • A reminder when a meeting in one of your calendars (Google, Outlook, iCloud or any ICS link) starts.
  • The optional Chrome extension's button in Meet and Zoom tabs.

Living in the tray

Closing the window keeps MeetMind running in the tray, ready for the next call, and it can start with Windows. With automatic updates turned on in Settings › General, new versions download in the background and install while MeetMind is idle; an indicator in the top bar shows when one is ready.

Storage and privacy

Recordings, transcripts and notes stay on your PC. Audio is sent only to the speech-to-text provider you chose and the transcript only to your notes model. Settings › Storage shows how much space recordings use and can delete old recordings automatically while keeping their notes and transcripts.

Frequently asked questions

Why does Windows say it protected my PC?

The installer isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen doesn't recognise it. Every release file carries a signed build attestation; check it with gh attestation verify MeetMind-Setup-x.y.z.exe --repo aslahtp/meetmind to confirm the file was built from the public source code.

Which Windows versions are supported?

Windows 10 and Windows 11, 64-bit.

Does it record my side of the call too?

Yes. It records your microphone and the system audio together, so the transcript includes everyone.

Is it really free?

Yes, the app is free and open source (GPL-3.0). You pay only the AI providers you connect, and their free tiers cover moderate use.
