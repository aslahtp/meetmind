Short answer

MeetMind is free, open-source software that records Microsoft Teams meetings from your computer's audio output and microphone. On Windows it notices when the Teams app starts using your microphone and offers to record. After the call it transcribes the recording with the engine you choose and writes minutes with decisions and action items, all stored on your computer.

How MeetMind records a Teams call

MeetMind doesn't connect to Teams at all. It captures what your computer plays (Windows loopback audio, Core Audio on macOS, PulseAudio or PipeWire on Linux) together with your microphone, so it hears everyone in the call the way you do. That means it works with:

  • the new Teams and classic Teams desktop apps on Windows;
  • Teams in Chrome, Edge, Brave, Firefox, Opera, Vivaldi or Arc;
  • work, school and personal Teams accounts, and meetings hosted by other organisations you join as a guest;
  • teams-for-linux, the community Teams client, on Linux.

Starting a recording

  1. Automatically (Windows and Linux): when Teams starts using your microphone, a small overlay offers to record. The Teams desktop app counts as soon as it uses the microphone; in a browser, MeetMind also looks for a Teams tab. When the call ends, the overlay asks whether to stop.
  2. From a calendar reminder: connect an Outlook or Google calendar with its ICS link, and MeetMind reminds you to record when a meeting starts.
  3. By hand: press Record in the app or the tray menu (the only way on macOS for now).

MeetMind or Teams' built-in recording?

Teams can record and transcribe meetings itself, but whether you can depends on your organisation's licences and policies, the organiser usually starts it, and recordings are saved to the organiser's OneDrive or SharePoint. Teams also announces the recording to everyone. MeetMind is useful when that isn't available to you, or when you want your own notes in a consistent format with your choice of AI model.

What you get

  • A transcript with speaker labels, from AssemblyAI, Google Cloud Speech-to-Text or Sarvam AI (Groq Whisper is faster and cheaper but doesn't label speakers).
  • Minutes written by Gemini, Groq or Claude: executive summary, agenda, key decisions and an action items table with owners and deadlines.
  • Every meeting searchable in one place, with PDF export, Markdown copy and optional Notion sync.

Ask before you record

Unlike Teams' own recording, MeetMind shows no banner to other participants. Tell them you are recording, and follow your organisation's policy and the law where you and they are: some places require everyone's consent.

Frequently asked questions

Is MeetMind a Teams bot?

No. It never joins the meeting and needs no Teams app, admin approval or Microsoft 365 permission. It records the audio on your computer.

Can I record a Teams meeting I don't organise?

MeetMind records whatever meeting you are in, but you should still get the organiser's and participants' agreement and follow your organisation's rules.

Does it work on Mac and Linux?

Yes, as betas. On macOS 14.2+, start recordings with Record. On Linux, MeetMind detects teams-for-linux and, on X11 sessions, Teams in a browser.

Does it cost anything?

The app is free and open source. You pay only for your own API usage, and the Gemini and Groq free tiers cover moderate use.
