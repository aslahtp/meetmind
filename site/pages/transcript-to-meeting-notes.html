Short answer

MeetMind, a free and open-source desktop app, extracts action items, decisions and a summary from meeting transcripts and recordings. Use Paste transcript for text you already have (only a notes key is needed: Gemini, Groq or Claude), or Import audio for a recording, which is transcribed first. The notes and transcript are saved on your computer.

From a transcript

  1. On the dashboard, choose Paste transcript.
  2. Paste the text from Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, a captioning tool or anywhere else.
  3. MeetMind skips speech-to-text and sends the text to your notes model, and the notes stream in as they are written.

From an audio or video file

Choose Import audio and pick a WAV, MP3, M4A, AAC, FLAC, OGG, Opus, WebM, MP4, MKV or MOV file. MeetMind converts it with the bundled FFmpeg, transcribes it with AssemblyAI, Google Cloud Speech-to-Text, Sarvam AI or Groq Whisper, and then writes the notes. Every engine except Groq labels the speakers.

What the notes contain

  • Executive summary: what the meeting was about and where it landed.
  • Agenda: the topics covered, in order.
  • Key decisions, numbered.
  • Action items: a table with each task's owner, deadline and priority.

Prefer data over prose? Switch to structured JSON notes, with identified speakers and prioritised action items. You can also edit the system prompt to change the format, language or level of detail.

Choose the model

Pick Gemini, Groq (Llama 3.3, GPT-OSS) or Claude, and a specific model for each. Set a fallback provider and model, and MeetMind retries with it if the first one fails, for example when a very long transcript is too big for Groq's free tier.

Is there a command-line tool?

No. MeetMind is a desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux, not a CLI. It is open source, though, and each AI provider is a small module, so the pipeline is easy to read and adapt: see the architecture overview.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a speech-to-text key to paste a transcript?

No. A pasted transcript only needs a notes key. The free tiers of Gemini and Groq are enough for most meetings.

Does it run the AI on my computer?

No. Transcription and notes use the cloud provider you choose, with your own key. What stays local is your data: recordings, transcripts and notes are stored on your computer, and there is no MeetMind server.

Can I export the notes?

Yes: as a PDF (optionally with the transcript), as Markdown, or as a Notion page with real headings, tables and to-dos.

Can it record new meetings too?

Yes, that's its main job: it records Zoom, Google Meet, Teams and any other call with no bot in the meeting.
