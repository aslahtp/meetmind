Short answer

To get Zoom meeting notes without Zoom's paid AI features or a bot, run MeetMind on the computer you join from. It records the call's audio and your microphone locally, then a speech-to-text engine of your choice writes the transcript and Gemini, Groq or Claude writes the minutes. The app is free and open source.

Starting a recording

  1. Automatically (Windows and Linux): when the Zoom desktop app starts using your microphone, MeetMind shows a small overlay with a Record button. Zoom in a browser counts too when a Zoom tab is open (on Linux, browser meetings need an X11 session).
  2. With the Chrome extension: a record button inside Zoom's web client.
  3. By hand: Record in the app or the tray menu, or the reminder for a meeting in your connected calendar.

When the meeting ends, MeetMind asks whether to stop rather than cutting the recording off, then processes it and shows the notes as they are written.

MeetMind or Zoom's own transcripts and AI Companion?

Zoom's cloud recording transcripts and AI Companion summaries need a paid Zoom plan, and the host's account settings decide whether they run. MeetMind works in any Zoom meeting you join, on a free account or as a guest in someone else's meeting, and keeps the result on your computer instead of the host's Zoom cloud.

What the notes contain

  • An executive summary and agenda of what was discussed.
  • Numbered key decisions.
  • An action items table with owners and deadlines.
  • The full transcript with speaker labels (AssemblyAI, Google Cloud Speech-to-Text or Sarvam AI).

Pick Markdown minutes or structured JSON, and edit the system prompt to change the format. For calls that mix English with Indic languages, Sarvam AI's saaras:v3 model handles the code-switching.

Ask before you record

MeetMind doesn't trigger Zoom's recording notice. Tell the other participants you are recording and follow the law where you and they are.

Frequently asked questions

Does MeetMind work with a free Zoom account?

Yes. It records audio on your computer, so it doesn't rely on any Zoom plan feature.

Does a bot join my Zoom meeting?

No. MeetMind adds no participant and needs no Zoom app or integration.

Can I get notes from a Zoom recording I already have?

Yes. Import the MP4 or M4A file, or paste Zoom's transcript, and MeetMind writes the notes. See turning a transcript or recording into notes.

Which systems does it run on?

Windows 10 and 11, and as betas macOS 14.2+ and Linux x64.
