MeetMind records your calls on your own computer, with no bot in the meeting, and writes the summary, decisions and action items with the AI provider you choose.

Short answer

Yes, there is a free alternative. MeetMind is a GPL-3.0 desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux. Instead of sending a bot into the call, it records the call's audio and your microphone on your computer, transcribes them with AssemblyAI, Google Cloud Speech-to-Text, Sarvam AI or Groq, and writes the notes with Google Gemini, Groq or Anthropic Claude using your own API keys. The app costs nothing; the free tiers of Gemini and Groq cover moderate use.

Why people look for an Otter.ai alternative

  • The bot in the call. A note-taker joining as a participant is visible to everyone, and some hosts remove unknown participants. MeetMind adds nothing to the meeting.
  • Per-seat subscriptions. MeetMind is free. You connect your own speech-to-text and AI keys, and one free Groq key is enough for both transcription and notes.
  • Data on someone else's servers. MeetMind keeps recordings, transcripts and notes on your computer. Audio goes only to the speech-to-text provider you pick, and the transcript only to the notes model you pick.
  • Language mix. Meetings that switch between English and Hindi, Tamil, Malayalam or other Indic languages mid-sentence are transcribed accurately with Sarvam AI's saaras:v3 model.
  • Control over the notes. Choose Markdown minutes or structured JSON, edit the system prompt, and pick the exact model, with automatic fallback to another provider if the first one fails.

How MeetMind works without a bot

  1. Record. Press Record, accept the prompt MeetMind shows when Google Meet, Zoom or Teams starts using your microphone (Windows and Linux), or use the optional Chrome extension's button in Meet and Zoom tabs.
  2. Transcribe. When you stop, the recording goes to your speech-to-text engine, which returns a transcript with speaker labels.
  3. Summarize. Your notes model writes an executive summary, agenda, key decisions and an action items table with owners and deadlines. You watch the notes appear as they are written.
  4. Share. Search every meeting, export a PDF, copy Markdown or sync the page to Notion.

When a hosted note taker is still the better choice

MeetMind is not a drop-in clone, and it is not the right tool for everyone. A hosted service such as Otter.ai or Fireflies.ai may suit you better if you need:

  • a live transcript while the meeting is still running (MeetMind transcribes after you stop);
  • notes from meetings you don't attend yourself (MeetMind records only on the computer it runs on);
  • a phone app, or a shared team workspace with comments and permissions;
  • no API keys to manage at all.

If what you want is accurate minutes from your own calls, no bot, no subscription and your data on your own machine, MeetMind is built for exactly that.

Moving your old meetings over

If your current tool lets you export recordings or transcripts, MeetMind can turn them into its own notes: use Import audio for WAV, MP3, M4A, MP4, WebM and other files, or Paste transcript to skip speech-to-text entirely. See turning a transcript or recording into meeting notes.

Frequently asked questions

Is MeetMind really free?

Yes. The app is free and open source under the GPL-3.0 license, with no paid tier. You pay only the AI providers you connect, and the Gemini and Groq free tiers and AssemblyAI's signup credit cover moderate use at no cost.

Does MeetMind join my Zoom, Google Meet or Teams call?

No. It records the audio your computer plays plus your microphone, so no extra participant appears. Because nothing announces the recording, tell the other participants that you are taking notes.

Is MeetMind private?

There is no MeetMind account or server. Recordings, transcripts and notes stay on your computer; audio is sent only to the speech-to-text provider you choose and the transcript only to the notes model you choose, under your own API keys and those providers' terms.

Does it work on Mac and Linux?

Yes, as betas: macOS 14.2 or later on Apple silicon and Intel, and Linux x64 as an AppImage or .deb. The Windows app supports Windows 10 and 11.
